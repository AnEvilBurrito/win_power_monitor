01

Familiar, not miniaturized.

Neutral numbers, monospace readings, one cool trace. Terminal palettes without terminal-sized touch targets.

02

The service is the product.

Leave the screen, keep the session. A persistent notification makes recording visible and stoppable.

BACKGROUND / NOTIFICATION CONCEPT

wWPM · Recording09:41
Low-impact monitoring

Last sample 09:41 · 4.80 W ↓ discharge

Illustrative only. Android controls the actual notification layout. Values carry a timestamp, not a promise of constant refresh.

03

Don't measure away the idle.

No always-on animation or wake lock by default. Missing intervals stay missing. Overhead claims wait for hardware measurements.

What to assess
  • Is watts → chart → session the right hierarchy?
  • Are the three navigation destinations enough?
  • Is the background mode clear without explanation?
  • Does it still feel like WPM?
