UI STUDY 02 — POWER DISTRIBUTION — NOT A WORKING APP

What the bars mean

Each reading is counted once into a watt bin, so a bar is percentage of counted discharge readings, not an average and not a distribution of instantaneous samples weighted by time.

  • Charging, idle, missing and gap-marked samples never enter a bin; the header reports how many were excluded.
  • Percentages divide by the counted population n, so they sum to 100%.
  • Fewer than 8 counted readings keeps the placeholder: collecting 3/8 readings.
  • An empty bin draws nothing. The hole is data, not a rendering bug.

Ported constants

MIN_SAMPLES 8 · MIN_BINS 6 · MAX_BINS 14 · LOG_SUPPORT_RATIO 10 · QUANTUM_DECIMALS 3 · NICE_PERCENT 5,10,15,20,25,30,40,50,60,75,100 · STEP_LADDER 1,1.5,2,2.5,3,4,5,7.5,10.

Bin width is Freedman–Diaconis, snapped to that ladder and to the meter's own increment; edges start below the first reading rather than at zero. A decade of support switches the axis to log-spaced, and the header says so.

Layout contract

The histogram reuses the chart's 44dp axis gutter and its axis+legend height budget (AXIS_AND_LEGEND_HEIGHT_DP 40), so the landscape canvas keeps its 120dp minimum and the portrait plot keeps 184dp.

The one added control costs 88dp in the toolbar budget: controls grow 374 → 468dp and the one-line caption minimum grows 506 → 600dp, still under the 720dp two-row threshold. Below 318dp of available portrait width the toggle takes its own 48dp row instead of squeezing the chips.

Interaction

  • Window chips scope the population: 5m / 30m / 2h change n and the bin width.
  • Absolute/signed changes the readout and the line chart; the distribution counts discharge magnitudes, so its bars and edges are identical in both scales.
  • Freeze bins the frozen snapshot: the bars hold still, the caption says the view is frozen.
  • Rotation and the selected view survive configuration change; the choice is not persisted across a process restart.
  • Bars are not tappable: a 14-bin plot on a 320dp phone is ~20dp per bar, below the 48dp target this app holds. The plot is one accessible summary node instead.
